## Part B (site repo, branch feat/screening-dashboard)

- `src/app/strategies/page.tsx`
  - Imports `getScreeningGrid` from `@/lib/screening` and `ScreeningGrid` from
    `@/components/ScreeningGrid`.
  - `getAllBotsWithStats()` and `getScreeningGrid()` now run via `Promise.all`.
  - New section titled « Ce que j'ai testé, et ce qui en reste », rendered ABOVE
    `<StrategiesClient bots={bots} />`, matching the page's existing `<section>` +
    `<h2 className="text-sm font-semibold text-muted uppercase tracking-widest mb-4">`
    markup convention.
  - The section is gated on `campaigns.length > 0`. Reason: with an empty array,
    `ScreeningGrid` does NOT render a bare/invisible shell — it renders a full
    3-cell corpus summary block (all zeros) plus a French sentence
    « J'ai jugé 0 configurations sur 0 stratégie. J'en ai rejeté 0. Il en reste 0 en
    observation. » That reads as a **false claim of completed measurement** (0 judged
    out of 0 strategies) rather than "nothing has been screened/exported yet", which
    is the actual state of an empty/never-exported table. Gating the section removes
    that false-shell risk entirely: when there are no campaigns, the section — and its
    misleading zero-copy — simply does not render, and the page falls back to exactly
    its pre-change behavior (bot list + FAQ only).
  - FAQ section and everything else left untouched.
- No `'use client'`, no hooks added — `StrategiesPage` stays an async server component.
- Test run: `npx vitest run --dir tests` → **404 passed (68 files)**, no navigation
